Avalon controls fan and work mode for Canaan Avalon miners; a change applies right away

MinerFan nowChips avg / maxHashrate
Miner auto: the miner runs its fan as slowly as it can until its chips average about 90 °C (quiet, but hot). Hold °C: the dashboard adjusts the fan once a minute to keep the chip average near that temperature (a few minutes to settle; at its 15% minimum fan a cool room can keep it below the target, and at 85 °C the fan goes to 100%). Fixed %: one fan speed. Cooler chips usually give a little more hashrate, for more fan noise. The dashboard must be running to hold a temperature; if the miner restarts, the dashboard sets the fan again. Changing the work mode makes the miner re-tune for a few minutes.

Solo45 settings

seconds
How often Solo45 sends fresh work with the latest transactions on the same block (1–120 s). New blocks are always sent instantly no matter what this is set to. Shorter picks up new fees sooner; longer means fewer job changes.
seconds (0 = off)
If your node has seen a new block announced but one peer still hasn't sent it after this long, Solo45 disconnects that peer so the node gets the block elsewhere. Bitcoin Core on its own waits 10 minutes, which your miners would spend on an old block.
Where a found block's reward goes. Your node checks the address before it's saved.
on a new block, miners get coinbase-only work at once, then the full template after s (1 to 40; applies from the next block)
On: every miner pays the address above, whatever its username says, so nobody else on your network can mine to their own address on your pool. Off: a miner with a Bitcoin address at the start of its username (address.name) pays that address instead, handy for a friend's miner.
per kWh
Used for the cost per day and per month. The power is what the miners report, which is usually a little below what they draw from the wall.
For a miner on another pool. Miners on Solo45 show up by themselves. The dashboard reads Bitaxe (AxeOS), Braiins OS, Thor OS, MARA and stock Bitmain firmware, and asks the miner first, so only an address that answers like a miner is saved.

Template policy which transactions go into blocks Solo45 builds; your node's own relay rules still apply first

TotalsBlocksSkippedFees skippedShare of feesWatch rules would skip
BlockTransactionsSkippedFees skippedWatch rulesWhy
Skipped transactions only give up their fees; the block reward itself doesn't change. A transaction that spends a skipped one is skipped too. The fee rule judges packages the way your node does, so a low-fee parent stays when its child pays enough for both. Watch rules only count what they would skip, so you can see what a rule would cost before switching it to Filter. Every filtered job is still checked by your node, and if a check ever fails Solo45 mines the node's own template for that block.

Phone alerts push notifications through the free ntfy app, no account needed

Your own ntfy server (optional)
1. Install ntfy on your phone (Android or iPhone). 2. In the app, tap + and subscribe to your topic. 3. Save here and press Send test. Anyone who knows the topic can read your alerts, so keep the random name private. Alerts only contain miner names, block heights and share sizes, never your address.

Backup & restore all your settings in one file, for a reinstall or a new Umbrel

The file holds your payout address and the "Always pay my address" switch, per-miner difficulty, template policy, job and stall-guard timing, electricity price, and phone-alert settings including the topic, so keep it private. It doesn't include an ntfy access token, statistics or logs. Restoring adds and replaces those settings; miners that aren't in the file keep theirs.
